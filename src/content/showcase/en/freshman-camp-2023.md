As **Vice Convener** of NCKU's 13th freshman orientation camp (成功登大人), my partner and I built a **300-person organizing team** over six months and planned a three-day program of talks, exhibitions and workshops to help **3,000 incoming students** start university life.

Days before the event, **Typhoon Haikui** hit. Within five days we condensed the three-day program into one day, coordinated closely with the university, adjusted the plan to the weather forecast and kept every participant safe. The event went ahead successfully.
