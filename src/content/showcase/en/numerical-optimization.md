## Overview

In the *Numerical Optimization* course I studied the mathematical models behind AI problems — the Weber problem, support vector machines and neural networks — and implemented the core algorithms myself in Python:

- Gradient descent
- Newton's method
- Conjugate gradient

Optimization is the underlying logic of machine learning, and this course (finished with an **A+**) became my entry point into AI research.
