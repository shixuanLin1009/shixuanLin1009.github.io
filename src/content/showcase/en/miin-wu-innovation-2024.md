Our team, **Linsanity**, entered the *Smart Computing Innovative Applications* competition hosted by the NCKU Miin Wu School of Computing with the [multi-object basketball player tracking system](../basketball-tracking/) and was selected as a **finalist in the Innovative Computing track**.

The system detects players with a YOLOv8x model trained on 700 hand-labeled P. LEAGUE+ images (six classes) and links them into trajectories with ByteTrack. It needs only a single broadcast view, which makes game data affordable for college and amateur teams that cannot pay for multi-camera tracking.

The project showed how detection and tracking models can move from benchmarks to a real cross-disciplinary use case: helping basketball players and coaches analyze games faster.

The entry was submitted to the 2nd *Grand Challenge: Innovative Cross-disciplinary Applications* under the title *Automatic Highlight Editing System for Multi-Object Basketball Offense*, and the finalist certificate was issued on August 17, 2024.
