Our team entered the *Smart Computing Innovative Applications* competition hosted by the NCKU Miin Wu School of Computing with the [multi-object basketball player tracking system](../basketball-tracking/) and was selected as a **finalist in the Innovative Computing track**.

The project showed how detection and tracking models can move from benchmarks to a real cross-disciplinary use case: helping basketball players and coaches analyze games faster.
