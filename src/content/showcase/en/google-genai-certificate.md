During the summer before my senior year I joined Google's Digital Talent Training Program and passed its assessment. The training covered two areas:

1. **Cloud computing** — Google Cloud infrastructure and machine-learning services, and how to design efficient AI systems in the cloud.
2. **Generative AI development** — building text- and image-generation applications with Google Cloud's AI tools.
