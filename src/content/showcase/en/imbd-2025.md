## Problem

During long machining runs, cutting heat makes lathe components expand. This thermal displacement can account for **40–70%** of the total loss in machining precision.

## Approach

For the *IMBD National Smart Manufacturing Big Data Analysis Competition* (hosted by the Ministry of Education), I adapted the **Temporal Fusion Transformer (TFT)** from the *International Journal of Forecasting*. Readings from **24 temperature sensors** placed around the lathe are the inputs, and the model predicts the physical displacement along the **X and Z axes**.

## Results

Training and validation were stable (train loss 4.97, validation loss 8.05), and the predictions closely followed the measured displacement trends on the validation set.
